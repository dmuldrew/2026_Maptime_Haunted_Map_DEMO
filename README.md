# The Haunted Map — European Folklore Behind Halloween Costumes

🎃 **Live Demo:** [https://dmuldrew.github.io/2026_Maptime_Haunted_Map_DEMO/](https://dmuldrew.github.io/2026_Maptime_Haunted_Map_DEMO/)

An interactive Leaflet-powered digital reproduction of a classic gothic poster map exploring the origins and folklore of iconic European Halloween creatures and costumes.

---

## 🗺️ Maptime Seattle Workshop

This project was created for and showcased at **Maptime Seattle**:

- **Event**: [**The Haunted Map: A Cartographic Journey Through the European Folklore Behind Familiar Halloween Costumes**](https://www.meetup.com/maptimesea/events/316191870/)
- **Date & Time**: Thursday, October 8, 2026 · 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM PDT
- **Location**: University of Washington — Bill & Melinda Gates Center for Computer Science & Engineering (UW CSE 2, Room G04), 3800 E Stevens Way NE, Seattle, WA
- **Organizers**: [MaptimeSEA](https://www.meetup.com/maptimesea/)
- **Venue Support**: [Taskar Center for Accessible Technology (TCAT)](https://tcat.cs.washington.edu/) at the University of Washington

### Workshop Focus:
The workshop guided participants through hands-on thematic cartography, exploring how to craft an eye-catching static poster map in QGIS and bring it to life on the web:
- **Folklore & Geographic Origins**: Tracing the roots of classic monsters and folklore figures across Europe (e.g., Frankenstein in Geneva, Dracula in Transylvania, werewolves in Livonia, witches of Pendle Hill, ghosts of Edinburgh, and the Headless Horseman/Dullahan in Ireland).
- **Cartographic Design Concepts**: Applying color theory, period-appropriate typography, composition balance, and visual hierarchy.
- **Interactive Companion Experience**: Transforming a static print layout into an animated, narratable web application featuring Leaflet, procedural Web Audio soundscapes, and text-to-speech synchronization.

## ✨ Features

- **Multi-Edition Gothic Poster Collection**:
  - 🏰 **Edition 1: European Gothic Folklore** — The classic European origins behind familiar Halloween costumes (Dullahan, Warwick Ghost, Trier Witches, Frankenstein, Werewolves of Valais, Krampus, Vlad Dracula).
  - 🌲 **Edition 2: North American Cryptids & Legends** — Indigenous spirits, Appalachian phantoms, and New World beasts (Bigfoot / Sasquatch, The Wendigo, The Flatwoods Monster, The Mothman, The Jersey Devil, El Chupacabra, El Silbón).
  - 🐉 **Edition 3: Old World Bestiary (Asia, Africa & Oceania)** — Ancient mythic creatures and primeval beasts (The Loch Ness Monster, Mokele-Mbembe, Popobawa, The Yeti, The Jiangshi, The Kappa, The Bunyip).
  - 🌐 **Edition 4: The Haunted World (Grand Worldwide Odyssey)** — An epic globe-spanning expedition connecting 14 legendary cryptids across six continents along an unbroken circumglobal spectral path.
- **Extended Global Cartography & Graticules**: Full planetary coverage across 258 countries rendered in the signature gothic dark palette (`#3f3f41` land, `#222327` sea) with global graticule grid lines.
- **Extended Ectoplasmic Paths & Continuous Journeys**: High-density glowing 4-layer spectral routes connecting stops on every continent with real-time distance interpolation and heading tracking.
- **Period-Accurate 19th-Century Woodcut Engravings**: High-resolution vintage Victorian bestiary plates and historical portraits for every cryptid stop.
- **Classic Poster & Edge-to-Edge Explorer Modes**: Seamlessly toggle between authentic Victorian poster layouts and interactive edge-to-edge exploration.
- **Narrated Tour & Spooky Voiceover**: Animated phantom marker with synchronized word-by-word text highlighting and customizable speech synthesis.
- **Atmospheric Gothic Soundscape**: Procedurally synthesized dark ambient drone, binaural pulsations, and church bell tolls via Web Audio.
- **Interactive Grimoire & Draggable Lore Cards**: Freely draggable cards with glowing phantom tethers, deep mythological archives, and lightbox galleries.

## 🚀 Deployment to GitHub Pages

This repository is configured for automated static deployment to **GitHub Pages** using GitHub Actions.

### One-time Repository Setup:
1. Go to your repository on GitHub: [`dmuldrew/2026_Maptime_Haunted_Map_DEMO`](https://github.com/dmuldrew/2026_Maptime_Haunted_Map_DEMO).
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment** → **Source**, select **GitHub Actions**.

### Automated Publishing:
- Pushing commits to `main` or `master` will trigger the automated [deploy workflow](.github/workflows/deploy.yml).
- You can also manually trigger a deployment at any time from the GitHub repository's **Actions** tab using `Run workflow`.
