## 🗺️ Maptime Seattle Workshop

This project was created for and showcased at **Maptime Seattle**:

- **Event**: [**The Haunted Map: A Cartographic Journey Through the European Folklore Behind Familiar Halloween Costumes**](https://www.meetup.com/maptimesea/events/316191870/)
- **Date & Time**: Thursday, October 8, 2026 · 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM PDT
- **Location**: University of Washington — Bill & Melinda Gates Center for Computer Science & Engineering (UW CSE 2, Room G04), 3800 E Stevens Way NE, Seattle, WA
- **Organizers**: [MaptimeSEA](https://www.meetup.com/maptimesea/)
- **Venue Support**: [Taskar Center for Accessible Technology (TCAT)](https://tcat.cs.washington.edu/) at the University of Washington

### Workshop Focus:
The workshop guided participants through hands-on thematic cartography, exploring how to craft an eye-catching static poster map in QGIS and bring it to life on the web:
- **Folklore & Geographic Origins**: Tracing the roots of classic monsters and folklore figures across Europe (e.g., Frankenstein in Geneva, Dracula in Transylvania, werewolves in Livonia, witches of Pendle Hill, ghosts of Edinburgh, and the Headless Horseman/Dullahan in Ireland).
- **Cartographic Design Concepts**: Applying color theory, period-appropriate typography, composition balance, and visual hierarchy.
- **Interactive Companion Experience**: Transforming a static print layout into an animated, narratable web application featuring Leaflet, procedural Web Audio soundscapes, and text-to-speech synchronization.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Classic Poster & Edge-to-Edge Explorer Modes**: Seamlessly toggle between the authentic Victorian poster layout and an interactive exploration map.
- **Narrated Tour & Eerie Voiceover**: Animated route with synchronized text highlighting and customizable spooky voice synthesis.
- **Atmospheric Gothic Soundscape**: Procedurally generated ambient drone, binaural pulsations, and vintage thunder crackles via the Web Audio API.
- **Interactive Lore Cards**: Draggable folklore cards with high-fidelity vintage illustrations, legends, and location pinpoints.
